## Brand & Style

### Personality & Brand Narrative
This design system embodies the ethos of "Less is more"—a deliberate sanctuary away from algorithmic noise, designed for readers who value depth, reflection, and the quiet dignity of the written word. It blends classic literary sensibility with contemporary typographic purity. The visual language evokes tactile parchment, deep mineral ink, and the passage of time.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
- **Audience:** Discerning readers, thinkers, journalers, and literary curators aged 18–45 who balance physical reading with thoughtful digital tracking.
- **Tone & Mood:** Meditative, refined, breathable, and timeless.
- **Emotional Response:** An instant sensation of calm deceleration; opening the platform feels like stepping into a private, sunlit library where every margin is generous and content breathes naturally.

### Design Movement
- **Minimalist Literary Editorial:** Radical whitespace, strict content hierarchy, subtle paper-warm tonal foundations, and deliberate elimination of ornamental UI friction.
- Subtle physical metaphors are translated purely through typography, hairline boundaries, and ambient illumination rather than skeuomorphic clutter.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in classical publishing aesthetics: natural uncoated paper and deep mineral pigment.

### Chromatic Roles & Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#2D4A43` - Deep Pine Ink):** Used for focal brand moments, active tab indicators, primary action buttons, key timeline nodes, and high-priority interactive touchpoints.
- **Secondary (`#C29B38` - Patina Gold):** Reserved for prestige states, reading milestone achievements, active streaks, member badges, and subtle data highlights in time pulse charts.
- **Tertiary (`#1A2421` - Book Ink):** The anchor for high-contrast reading text, key book titles, and editorial quotes.
- **Neutral (`#F7F7F5` - Soft Parchment Warm White):** Forms the global foundation, eliminating harsh digital glare while honoring paper texture.

### Surface and Foreground System
- The page background sits on `paper-base` (`#FBFBFA`) and `paper-warm` (`#F7F7F5`).
- Surface elevations use `paper-elevated` (`#FFFFFF`) with ultra-fine borders (`border-hairline` `#EAE8E3`).
- Text uses a calibrated tripartite ink hierarchy: `ink-primary` (`#1A2421`) for titles and body, `ink-secondary` (`#4A5451`) for metadata, and `ink-muted` (`#8A9490`) for timestamps, labels, and placeholders.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical literary authority with functional precision:

- **Editorial Headings & Book Titles (`Noto Serif` / 思源宋体):** Imbued with character, balance, and literary dignity. Used across all section titles, curated book names, author names, and pull quotes.
- **Reading Body, Data, and System UI (`Inter` / 苹方 / 思源黑体):** Uncluttered and rational. Body copy has a relaxed line-height ratio (1.7–1.8) and slight positive letter-spacing to prevent visual fatigue during sustained reading sessions.
- **Data & Reading Pulse Visuals:** Numeric milestones (e.g., reading minutes, page counters, heatmap intervals) maintain tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) for clean columnation.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
A generous fixed-width fluid container system that strictly limits content line-lengths to optimize cognitive ease. Long-form reviews and reading logs conform to a dedicated `reading-column-max` (680px), preserving ideal line lengths of 60–75 characters.

### Grid Model
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column grid with 32px gutters and a 1200px max container centered with auto margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 24px gutters and 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px fluid side paddings.

### Rhythm & Whitespace
Vertical rhythm relies on progressive whitespace: 
- Grouped items: `space-sm` (12px) to `space-md` (16px).
- Independent modular cards: `space-xl` (32px).
- Major thematic segments and pulse reports: `space-3xl` (72px) to `space-4xl` (96px). 
Ample whitespace is treated as an intentional compositional element, guiding focus directly onto book covers, reading durations, and prose.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows the physical metaphor of stacked sheets of warm art paper in soft ambient daylight:

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure base `paper-base` (`#FBFBFA`), zero shadow, raw paper presence.
- **Surface Level 1 (Resting Card / Container):** `paper-elevated` (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate hairline boundary (`1px solid #EAE8E3`) and a whisper-light ambient drop shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px -2px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.03), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Timer / Hovered Book Card):** Lifted slightly higher for active states: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Modals / Drawers):** Supported by a warm backdrop blur: `backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(251, 251, 250, 0.85); border: 1px solid rgba(234, 232, 227, 0.8)`.
- **Philosophy:** No artificial heavy drop shadows or harsh contrast lines. Boundaries define structure; ambient light conveys hierarchy.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic softness and typographic discipline using a `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem base radius) foundation:

- **Interactive Buttons, Chips, and Input Fields:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius for a calm, friendly, tactile response.
- **Content Cards, Modal Sheets, and Book Presentation Panels:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) corner radius to create gentle contours without feeling overly playful or juvenile.
- **Book Covers:** Standardized `0.25rem` (4px) micro-rounding with an authentic spine drop-shadow on the left edge.
- **Reading Pulse Dot Nodes & Status Indicators:** Fully circular (`rounded-full`) to represent continuity and organic pulse beats.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background `pine-deep` (`#2D4A43`), text `paper-elevated` (`#FFFFFF`), `0.5rem` border-radius, font weight 500. Hover: background `#243C36`, subtle translateY(-1px). Active: translateY(0).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #EAE8E3`, text `ink-primary` (`#1A2421`). Hover: background `paper-warm` (`#F7F7F5`), border color `#D0CFC9`.
- **Text Link Button:** No border, text `pine-deep` (`#2D4A43`), underlined on hover with a 2px offset.

### Chips & Filters
- **Filter Chip (Default):** Background `paper-warm` (`#F7F7F5`), border `1px solid #EAE8E3`, text `ink-secondary` (`#4A5451`), padding 6px 14px, radius 8px.
- **Filter Chip (Selected):** Background `pine-subtle` (`#E8EFEA`), border `1px solid #2D4A43`, text `pine-deep` (`#2D4A43`), font weight 500.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- **Input Style:** Background `paper-elevated` (`#FFFFFF`), border `1px solid #EAE8E3`, color `ink-primary`, placeholder `ink-muted`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `pine-deep` (`#2D4A43`), outer halo `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(45, 74, 67, 0.08)`. Zero jarring outlines.

### Reading Cards
- **Book Display Card:** Clean card with 16px vertical padding, no heavy containment borders; relies on whitespace and a clean `1px solid #EAE8E3` bottom divider or elevated surface.
- **Book Spine Treatment:** 3:4 aspect ratio with `box-shadow: 2px 4px 12px rgba(26, 36, 33, 0.08)`.

### Reading Pulse Timeline & Visualizations (Core Differentiator)
- **Pulse Heatmap Calendar:** Square blocks (12px × 12px, 3px gap, 2px border radius). Intensity scales from `#F0EFEA` (0 mins) through 3 tint levels of `#2D4A43` up to deep `#1D322D` (120+ mins).
- **Session Waveform / Duration Curve:** 2px smoothed stroke (`#2D4A43`) over an ultra-faint gradient fill (`rgba(45, 74, 67, 0.06)` fading to `transparent`). Milestones are pinned with a glowing `gold-patina` (`#C29B38`) ring node.

### Floating Session Timer (Global Persistent Widget)
- **Appearance:** Bottom-pinned pill panel, frosted glass background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` + blur 12px), bordered by `border-hairline`, displaying book thumbnail, ticking time (`tabular-nums`), and a minimal play/pause/stop toggle in `pine-deep`.
